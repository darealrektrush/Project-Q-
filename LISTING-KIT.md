## Trending tips (free)

- **Batch it.** Do all 23 in one session with the Master Data block open — most
  reuse the exact same fields.
- **Rally votes.** For vote boards, drop the vote link in your Telegram/X and
  ask holders to vote daily. Coordinated daily votes are what put you on the
  front page people actually browse.
- **Lean on the angle.** On rugfreecoins.com and any "safety" board, your
  on-chain receipts + fixed 75/15/10 split + ocean 10% is a genuinely strong,
  verifiable pitch most listings can't make.
- **Track it.** Tick the boxes here as you go so you don't double-submit.
